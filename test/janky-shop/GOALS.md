# Performance goals

1. `scroll`: browsing the grid is most of a visit.
2. `open-cart`: opening the cart must feel instant.
3. `load`: the first seconds, on a phone-class machine.

## What counts as a modest machine?

- Throttle: 4x

## What won't you trade away?
